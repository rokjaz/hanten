ASK

At the loneliest spot in the ocean, where are the nearest people?

Point Nemo is the spot in the ocean farthest from land.

Who might be closer than you expect?
PREDICT

Who might sometimes be closest to you at Point Nemo?

Imagine you are floating at Point Nemo with no ships nearby.

Choose one before you reveal the answer.
REVEAL

The closest people can sometimes be in orbit.

At Point Nemo, “up” can be closer than “away.”
The ISS is not always directly over Point Nemo. This compares its orbital altitude with Point Nemo’s surface distance to the nearest land.
UNDERSTAND

Where does “space” belong on the same distance scale?

Move the marker to where you think the ISS altitude belongs. You can place it beyond the nearest land if that is your guess.
For reference: a typical commercial jet cruises only about 10–12 km / 6–7 mi above Earth.

TRANSFER

What makes something feel “far away”?

The ISS is about 254 miles above Earth.
Does that sound far?
A spacecraft does not travel straight up; it climbs into orbit and lines up with the moving International Space Station.
Now try four ordinary examples. What is making each one feel “far”?
“My grandparents live 250 miles away.”
“The airport is an hour away.”
“The summit is only three miles away.”
“The other side of the canyon is only one mile away—but there’s no bridge.”
Next time something sounds “far away,” ask:
Distance?   Travel time?   Difficulty / effort?
The same place can feel close by one measure and far by another.
