UNDERSTAND

Same Earth. Different map. Different sacrifice.

A flat map cannot preserve everything at once. Compare three legitimate ways to flatten the same world.

Preserve AREAEqual Earth
Relative area is trustworthy. Shapes bend.
Preserve LOCAL SHAPEMercator
Local angles and shapes work well. Area explodes toward the poles.
DISTANCE FROM A CENTERAzimuthal equidistant
Distance and direction from the center are useful. Other shapes stretch.
The Earth did not change. The mapmaker's priority did.
