UNDERSTAND

Land and people tell very different stories.

Land is enormous

Large parts of the country have relatively low population density. They dominate a conventional land-area map.

People cluster

Dense metropolitan counties occupy comparatively little land while containing tens of millions of people.

A map can show where land is without showing where most people are.
