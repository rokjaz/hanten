What is an APRUT?

An APRUT is a short, interactive classroom activity built around five steps. Students commit to a prediction before seeing what happens, discover the idea, understand why it works, and then apply it somewhere new.

ASK→PREDICT→REVEAL→UNDERSTAND→TRANSFER

No prep. About five minutes. Built for discussion.
