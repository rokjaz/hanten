One idea. Three ways to experience it.

Use it with a class, around a table, or across a room.

The pack gives you three ready-to-use formats built around the same Hanten discovery. Choose the version that fits your group—or use all three in different settings.

01

Classroom / presentation

APRUT

A short guided experience built around Ask → Predict → Reveal → Understand → Transfer. Participants commit to an idea before the explanation appears.

Approx. 5–10 minutes

02

Individual / small group

Experience Card

A hands-on version for ranking, testing, revealing, and discussing the three mountains without needing a presentation.

Table-ready activity

03

Workshop / meeting / group

Icebreaker

Place three mountain signs around the room. Everyone chooses by moving to an answer—then decides whether to move when the question changes.

Physical participation

What happens?

They make the discovery. You don’t have to give a lecture.

The materials are designed to create the moment of commitment, comparison, and reconsideration without putting the answer on the sales page—or making participants feel caught being wrong.
