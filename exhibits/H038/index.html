Where is the border in Kashmir?

The same mountain region can look different depending on what a map means by its boundary lines.

Indian-map treatment

Shown as Indian

Indian government maps depict Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh as Indian territory rather than presenting the territorial claim as unsettled.

Pakistani-map treatment

Marked contested

Pakistani maps use distinct boundary treatment to signal that the region’s final status is disputed rather than settled.

International-map treatment

Line of Control

Many international maps distinguish the Line of Control from a settled international border with a dashed or disputed-boundary symbol.

The mountains do not move. The political meaning of the line does. India, Pakistan, and China administer different parts of the wider Kashmir region, while territorial claims overlap. The India–Pakistan Line of Control is not the same thing as a mutually accepted final international border.
