Does Alaska cross into the Eastern Hemisphere?

The Aleutian Islands run so far west from mainland Alaska that the chain crosses the 180th meridian — the line that splits the globe into east and west — and keeps going.

180th meridian Eastern Hemisphere Western Hemisphere

Alaska is the westernmost, easternmost, and northernmost US state. Amatignak Island (179°08′55″W) is the westernmost point in the US by longitude. Keep going west along the same island chain and, instead of running out of islands, you cross 180° and start counting east again — landing on Semisopochnoi Island (179°46′23″E), the easternmost point in the US by longitude.

Both islands are uninhabited specks in the Aleutians, roughly 40 km apart on the same island chain — yet by the numbers, one is the most western point in the country and the other is the most eastern.
