Can a single Indian state outpopulate most countries on Earth?

Uttar Pradesh isn't a country — it's one of India's 28 states. Line it up against actual countries by population and it lands in the same league as Pakistan, ahead of Nigeria, Brazil, and Japan.

China and India themselves are off this chart entirely — at 1.4 billion each, they'd stretch it more than five times wider.

"Most populous country" and "most populous place" aren't the same list.

Uttar Pradesh, India's most populous state, holds an estimated 243 million people — more than Nigeria, Brazil, Bangladesh, Russia, Mexico, or Japan, each counted as a whole country. Guangdong, China's most populous province, holds about 128 million — more than Japan, a G7 economy, on its own. Both provinces are administrative subdivisions of countries so large that their internal pieces rival entire nations elsewhere.

"Biggest in the world" depends entirely on what unit you're comparing.
