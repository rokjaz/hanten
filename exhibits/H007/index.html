How Big Is Africa... Really?

Three of the world’s largest countries fit inside Africa — with room still left over.

Africa total 30.4M km²
United States (lower 48) 8.1M km²
China 9.6M km²
India 3.3M km²
Still uncovered ≈9.4M km²

United States + China + India all fit inside Africa.
About 9.4 million km² is still uncovered. Familiar world maps can make Africa feel smaller because many common projections enlarge places farther from the equator.
