Did the United States grow one state at a time?

Put the order of admission onto the map — then look at the timing.

Earlier Later
1912 Arizona becomes the 48th state
47 YEARS →
1959 Alaska, then Hawaii
The sequence looks orderly. The timing was not.
The original 13 entered in less than three years. After Arizona became No. 48 in 1912, the country waited 47 years for Alaska — then only seven and a half months for Hawaii.
