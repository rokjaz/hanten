How long would it take to drive to the International Space Station?

Translate 408 km into two distances that are easier to picture.

Drive straight up at 65 mph 3 hr 54 min

The ISS orbits about 408 km (254 miles) above Earth.

Shrink Earth to a basketball 7.6 mm

That's how far above the ball's surface the ISS would orbit.

The ISS is in space, but it is not very far from Earth. At ordinary highway speed, the imaginary trip takes less than four hours. Shrink Earth to a basketball and low Earth orbit is only a pencil-width away.

What sounds impossibly far away until you translate it into a familiar scale?

See the assumptions behind the comparison

The driving analogy assumes a constant 65 mph on an imaginary road straight upward. It ignores atmosphere, gravity, orbital mechanics, traffic, and the fact that the ISS is moving. The basketball comparison scales Earth's 12,742 km mean diameter to an official-size basketball about 9.4 inches across.

Source basis from the original H020 exhibit: ISS altitude 408 km; Kármán line 100 km; Earth's mean diameter 12,742 km; driving comparison at 65 mph.
