Greenland looks almost as large as Africa on many world maps. How many Greenlands actually fit inside Africa?

Put Greenland and Africa on the same equal-area scale.

Africa ≈ 30.4 million km² · Greenland ≈ 2.17 million km² · Ratio ≈ 14:1
Africa is about 14 times larger than Greenland.
Familiar world maps can make them look surprisingly similar because high-latitude land is greatly enlarged.
