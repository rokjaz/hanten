Did Cleopatra live closer in time to the pyramids or to the Moon landing?

We file the Great Pyramid and Cleopatra together under "ancient Egypt," and T. rex and Stegosaurus together under "dinosaurs." The gaps inside those labels are bigger than the gaps between them and us.

Human history

The Great Pyramid, Cleopatra, and the Moon landing on one timeline

Deep time

Stegosaurus, T. rex, and today, in millions of years ago

"Ancient" and "prehistoric" pack very different spans of time into one word. When Cleopatra ruled, the Great Pyramid was already about 2,500 years old, a longer gap than the roughly 2,000 years between her and Apollo 11. Stegosaurus had been extinct for at least 77 million years before the first T. rex appeared, more than the 66 million years since T. rex.

Labels like "the ancient world" or "the age of dinosaurs" feel like single moments because we learn them as single chapters. Before comparing two things filed under the same label, put them on the same timeline.

Try it somewhere else

Woolly mammoths and the Great Pyramid both feel like they belong to the distant past.

Were woolly mammoths still alive when the pyramid was built?

More time that hides inside one word

Yes: a small population of woolly mammoths survived on Wrangel Island, off Siberia, until about 4,000 years ago, several centuries after the Great Pyramid was finished. Oxford University was already teaching students in 1096, more than two centuries before the Aztec city of Tenochtitlan was founded in 1325. And the Roman Empire lasted longer in the east, through Constantinople until 1453, than the United States has existed.
