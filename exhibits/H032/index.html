Persian Gulf or Arabian Gulf?

The same body of water appears under different names on different maps.

The geography stays the same. The label depends on who made the map.

Explore the naming dispute

Why “Persian Gulf”?

The name has a long historical record and remains the standard terminology used by the United Nations and most international geographic references.

Why “Arabian Gulf”?

Several Arab states use “Arabian Gulf” in government, media, and mapping as their preferred regional name. The disagreement is about naming and identity, not the physical geography.
