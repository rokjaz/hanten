Is Paris north of Toronto, Montreal, and Ottawa?

Europe reads as roughly level with southern Canada on a mental map. It sits much farther north — Paris alone is above nearly every major Canadian city outside British Columbia and the Prairies.

Europe's cities cluster much farther north than people expect. The same mismatch shows up across the continent: London (51.5°N) sits north of every Canadian provincial capital except Edmonton. Europe's mild climate at such high latitude — courtesy of the Gulf Stream — is exactly what breaks the instinct that links latitude to weather.

The estimate above is built from 2021 census province populations, apportioned by whether each province's population is concentrated north or south of 48.86°N. Ontario, Quebec, and Atlantic Canada — home to most Canadians — sit almost entirely south of Paris; Alberta, the Prairies, and most of British Columbia's population sit north of it.
