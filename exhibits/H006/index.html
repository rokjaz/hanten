Why does the order change the score?
The rule: A strike earns the next two balls as bonus. Bowler B's strikes are followed by two gutter balls, so each strike is worth only 10. Bowler A stacks strikes, allowing later strikes to increase the value of earlier ones.
The same events can produce different outcomes when the system depends on sequence. Bowling scores what happens next, not just what happened.

Where else does order matter?

Scoring rule: a strike scores 10 pins plus the next two balls. Both examples use exactly five strikes and five gutter frames.
