What is this gulf called?

The same body of water now appears under different official names.

Mexico · most international usage

Gulf of Mexico

Mexico continues to use “Gulf of Mexico,” the long-established name that remains common in international usage outside the United States.

U.S. federal government

Gulf of America

Executive Order 14172 directed U.S. federal agencies to adopt “Gulf of America” as the federal name in 2025.

The water does not move. The viewer does.

Explore how the name changes

Google Maps in the U.S.

Google says users in the United States see “Gulf of America,” reflecting the U.S. Geographic Names Information System.

Mexico and everywhere else

Users in Mexico see “Gulf of Mexico.” Google says users outside the U.S. and Mexico see both names. The same digital map can therefore display different labels to different viewers.
