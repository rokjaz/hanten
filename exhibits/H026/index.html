    Cinderella stories

    #11 Texas — Texas survived the First Four, then upset #6 BYU 79–71 to reach the Round of 32 before continuing deep enough to become one of the state's last two survivors.

    #9 Iowa — Iowa knocked out South Region #1 seed Florida 73–72 and was still alive in the Elite Eight before Illinois ended the run.

    #12 High Point and #11 VCU — Both double-digit seeds produced first-round upsets, beating Wisconsin and North Carolina respectively.

    Upsets & turning points

    Texas goes from seven to zero. No state placed more teams in the field, but all seven Texas entries were eliminated before the Elite Eight.

    Duke falls by one. UConn beat Duke 73–72 in the Elite Eight, ending North Carolina's final remaining bid.

    Michigan finishes the funnel. After routing Tennessee 95–62, Michigan beat Arizona 91–73 and then UConn 69–63 for the national championship.
