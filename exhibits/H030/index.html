Why does Hawaii have interstate highways?

Hawaii's nearest neighboring state is about 2,400 mi (3,860 km) away across the Pacific, yet the island of O‘ahu has four interstate highways. None of them could ever cross a state line.

What the name suggests
A highway that crosses from one state to another
What it actually means
Part of the national “Interstate and Defense Highways” system, built mostly with federal money
O‘ahu's interstates, about 55 mi (89 km) in all

“Interstate” describes the system, not each road. The highways' full official name is the Dwight D. Eisenhower National System of Interstate and Defense Highways. A road belongs to it because the federal government designated it and paid most of the cost, 90% in the system's early decades, not because it crosses a state line. Hawaii's routes were approved in 1960, a year after statehood, and they tie together the island's military bases: Pearl Harbor, Hickam, Schofield Barracks, and the Marine Corps base at Kāne‘ohe.

Hawaii isn't even unusual. Several mainland interstates never leave their state, including I-4 in Florida, I-45 in Texas, and I-97 in Maryland. A name tells you where something came from, not always what it is. Before trusting what a label says, ask how it's actually defined.

The most expensive interstate ever built

H-3 cost about $80 million a mile. Approved in 1960 to connect Pearl Harbor with the Marine Corps base on the other side of the Ko‘olau mountains, it took 37 years of planning, lawsuits, and construction to open in 1997. Its final cost was $1.3 billion for 15.3 mi (24.7 km), making it the most expensive interstate ever built per mile, with tunnels through the mountains and long viaducts over valleys.

Alaska and Puerto Rico have interstates too

Alaska and Puerto Rico also have roads that count as interstates for federal funding, but they aren't signed as interstates and don't have to meet the same design standards. Hawaii's routes are the only signed interstates outside the lower 48 states, which is why H-1 is both the southernmost and the westernmost signed interstate in the country.
