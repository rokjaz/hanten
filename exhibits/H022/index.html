What are you counting — and is it actually what determines the outcome?

See why hits and runs measure different things

A hit helps create scoring opportunities, but a hit is not itself a run. Walks, extra-base hits, sequencing, outs, and other events affect how many runs a team produces. In this game, New York had more hits while Pittsburgh scored the extra run that decided the game.

Game 7, 1960 World Series: New York Yankees 13 hits, 9 runs; Pittsburgh Pirates 11 hits, 10 runs.
